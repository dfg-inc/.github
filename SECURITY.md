# Security Policy

We take the security of Praxis and every DFG repository seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report privately via one of:

- GitHub's private vulnerability reporting — the **Report a vulnerability** button on the affected repository's *Security* tab (preferred).
- Email: `info@dfuture.co`

Include, where possible: the affected repository and version, steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within **3 business days**.
- We keep you informed while we investigate and fix.
- We credit reporters who wish to be named once a fix is released.

Thank you for helping keep Praxis and its users safe.

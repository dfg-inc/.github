# Code of Conduct

Praxis and every Digital Future Group (DFG) repository under this organization follow the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, as the standard for participation.

## Our standard

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in full. It sets the expectation for respectful, harassment-free participation across issues, discussions, pull requests, and any other space connected to a DFG repository — regardless of experience level, background, or role.

## Scope

This code applies within all DFG-owned repositories and in any public space where someone represents the organization or one of its projects, including issue trackers, discussions, code review, and community channels tied to those repositories.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `business@dfuture.co`. Every report is reviewed and answered. We do not disclose a reporter's identity without their consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard, and they may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with it. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) — a graded response, from a private warning to a permanent ban, matched to the severity and pattern of the behavior.

# Support

Need help with Praxis, or want to talk to the team behind it? Here's where to go.

## Questions & help

- **Documentation & overview** — start at [dfuture.co](https://dfuture.co).
- **Bugs & feature requests** — open an issue in the relevant repository.
- **Questions & discussion** — use the repository's *Discussions* tab where enabled.

## Talk to us

Praxis is built by **DFG (Digital Future Group)**. To reach the team directly, write to `business@dfuture.co`.

We read everything and reply.
